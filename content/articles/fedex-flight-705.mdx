On 7 April 1994, FedEx employee Auburn Calloway attacked the operating crew of Flight 705 after departure from Memphis. Court records say all three crew members were seriously injured; the aircraft returned to Memphis. Calloway was later convicted of attempted aircraft piracy and related offenses.

## What happened

Captain David Sanders, First Officer James Tucker and Flight Engineer Andrew Peterson resisted after Calloway attacked them with hammers and a spear gun. Tucker initially remained at the controls while Sanders and Peterson fought Calloway; Sanders later returned to the cockpit and landed the aircraft in Memphis.

## Why this record matters

The case raises a specific security question: how should an airline assess access by its own employees? Crew survival, cockpit access and the evidence presented in court are separate parts of that question; none makes the attack an inevitable consequence of trust.
