- the publication homepage/product once public;
- already-published records that pass the launch selection;
- RSS;

- partnership conversations about the publication itself.

Do **not** syndicate the 42 review drafts.

## First outreach package, when authorized

For a qualifying outlet:
1. read at least five recent pieces from that outlet;
2. choose one story with genuine editorial fit;
3. confirm current AI/submission policy;
4. prepare a publication-specific pitch rather than a mass template;
5. disclose relevant provenance when required;
6. record the state and response;
7. never submit the same commissioned piece in conflict with exclusivity terms.
