On 25 March 1911, fire broke out at the Triangle Waist Company in New York City. The company occupied the top three floors of the Asch Building; 146 people died.

## What happened

The U.S. Department of Labor’s historical account describes the factory fire and the New York Factory Investigating Commission that followed. The commission examined workplace conditions across industries, and its work informed changes to the state’s labor and safety laws.
