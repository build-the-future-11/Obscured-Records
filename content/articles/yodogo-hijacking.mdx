In March 1970, nine members of the Red Army Faction hijacked Japan Airlines Flight 351 from Tokyo to Fukuoka and took refuge in North Korea, according to Japan’s National Police Agency.

## What happened

The agency’s 2019 history identifies the Yodo-go group separately from the Japanese Red Army. Its brief account also cites testimony given in 2002 concerning later abductions. That retrospective testimony is a separate evidentiary stage from the 1970 hijacking, not a current legal finding about every person involved.

## Why this record matters

The hijacking and the later allegations require different records. A police retrospective identifies the agency’s account; a fuller chronology needs contemporary records and independent reporting. It cannot establish every weapon, stop or negotiation in the journey.
