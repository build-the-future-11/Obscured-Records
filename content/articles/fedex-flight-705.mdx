On 7 April 1994, FedEx employee Auburn Calloway attacked the operating crew of Flight 705 after departure from Memphis. The three injured crew members fought back and returned the DC-10 to the airport. Prosecutors said Calloway intended to disguise the attack as an accident.

## What happened

Captain David Sanders, First Officer James Tucker and Flight Engineer Andrew Peterson resisted through violent dives and rolls that pushed the DC-10 beyond its certified limits. Tucker, suffering severe head injuries, used the cargo jet itself as a weapon—banking and diving to throw the attacker off balance—before the crew subdued Calloway and landed back at Memphis.

## Why this record matters

The case raises a specific security question: how should an airline assess access by its own employees? Crew survival, cockpit access and the evidence presented in court are separate parts of that question; none makes the attack an inevitable consequence of trust.
