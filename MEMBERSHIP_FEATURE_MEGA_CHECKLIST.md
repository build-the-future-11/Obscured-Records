# Membership feature mega checklist

## Product discovery and operating model

Obscured Records is an evidence-led publication about overlooked historical events, institutional failures, and the primary records needed to understand them. Its existing member product was a private reading library with annotations; contributor intake already supports corrections, sources, rights notes, pitches, and contributor introductions. The portal must therefore make responsible participation in research and editorial work easier, not invent a paid tier, a job board, credentials, or publication promises.

Member types are: **reader-member** (private library and opt-in participation), **contributor** (applies to real research/editorial calls), **project lead** (a member who created a project and reviews team join requests), **mentor** (a separately opt-in member), and **editorial administrator** (the server-authorized publication owner). Project lead and mentor are capabilities within a member account, not browser-asserted roles. No directory email is exposed.

Status key: **VERIFIED** = exercised against a working deployment and its backing store; **IMPLEMENTED-UNVERIFIED** = built, typechecked and linted but not runnable end-to-end here because this checkout has no configured D1 binding or ChatGPT-authenticated test identity; **BLOCKED** = requires policy, operations, or a real program owner; **NOT STARTED** = intentionally not claimed.

## 21 October completion pass (supersedes implementation statuses above)

The remaining code paths now exist: project invitations and confirmations, team milestones/evidence/messages, cohort tasks and materials, private feedback with author-only visibility, reasoned moderation and audit history, atomic event capacity/waitlist promotion, opt-in in-app reminders, ordered learning prerequisites, verified attendance, role revocation, owner-scoped export, and editorial intake links. **Local evidence:** actual route handlers and the pinned Drizzle D1 driver execute SQL against an isolated SQLite database with all migrations. Sixteen workflow scenarios cover identity/origin rejection, stable review decisions, transaction rollback, capacity, account isolation, prerequisite checks, enrollment privacy, moderation roles, mentor feedback visibility, invitation recipient checks, directory consent, attendance evidence, role revocation, throttling and credit confirmation. This is LOCAL-VERIFIED engineering evidence; it does not certify ChatGPT identity forwarding, hosted D1, real multi-account browser behavior, or every acceptance criterion in the original table.

Items previously marked NOT STARTED now have local implementation within these explicit boundaries:

- 26–29 and 71: lead-assigned outcome links and responsibilities stay private; members confirm their own credits. Milestone controls, invitations, team evidence and project messages are connected.
- 36–37 and 85: sessions, materials, tasks and personal task progress require accepted enrollment. Withdrawal uses the published enrollment deadline as its cutoff. A real facilitator and approved curriculum are still operational prerequisites for a real cohort.
- 42–44: mentor decisions and shared session history work; feedback is visible only to its author, as explained in the interface and privacy page. Account retention/deletion ownership remains an open project acceptance gate.
