In Minamata, Japan, families dependent on seafood began experiencing severe neurological illness. Animals had shown warnings first: cats convulsed, birds fell and fish floated.

## What happened

Industrial wastewater containing methylmercury entered the bay, concentrated through the food chain and reached people through fish and shellfish. The link was contested while exposure continued, compounding illness across generations.

## Why this record matters

Minamata is a record of delayed recognition. Pollution moved biologically while responsibility moved bureaucratically. The city’s name now belongs to the international convention on mercury—a treaty built from a disaster authorities took too long to stop.
