Two men entered an abandoned clinic in Goiânia, Brazil, and removed part of a radiotherapy machine for scrap. Inside was a capsule of cesium-137. When it was breached, the material emitted a blue glow that drew people closer.

## What happened

Fragments travelled through families and neighbourhoods before the source was recognized. Four people died, hundreds were contaminated or exposed, buildings were demolished and thousands of cubic metres of radioactive waste were generated.

## Why this record matters

Goiânia changed the language of radiological safety. The central danger was not a power station but an orphan source: useful technology that had slipped outside every institution responsible for it.
