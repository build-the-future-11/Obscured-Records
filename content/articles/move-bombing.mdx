After a day-long confrontation with members of MOVE, Philadelphia police dropped an explosive device on the roof of a row house in a densely built residential block.

## What happened

The resulting fire was allowed to burn. Eleven people, including five children, died; more than 60 neighbouring homes were destroyed. A city commission later found the decision to use the bomb unconscionable.

## Why this record matters

The bombing resists the categories that make events easy to remember. It was a police operation, an urban fire, a political conflict and a mass-casualty government action at once. The fractured label has helped fracture the public record.
