## Findings supported by the Commission chapter

- The Commission called the launch decision flawed.
- It found final decision-makers were unaware of important prior O-ring/joint problems.
- They were unaware of Thiokol's initial written recommendation against launch below 53°F and of continuing engineer opposition after management reversed its position.
- The Commission found failures in communication and a management structure that allowed flight-safety problems to bypass key Shuttle managers.
- The chapter documents different perceptions of the prelaunch teleconferences; testimony must not be flattened into a single recollection.
- The Commission concluded that Thiokol management reversed its position and recommended launch at Marshall's urging and contrary to its engineers' views.

## Testimony versus Commission finding

The “burden of proof” framing comes from witness testimony, including Roger Boisjoly and Robert Lund. It is useful evidence about how participants experienced the decision process, but the article must identify it as testimony rather than silently convert it into a Commission finding.
