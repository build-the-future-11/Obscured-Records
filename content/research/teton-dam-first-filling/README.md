# Teton Dam evidence packet

AI-assisted archival analysis awaiting human review. Four official documents distinguish the physical failure, operational controls, recovery and later institutional response. The 1976 panel PDF was downloaded and its summary/transmittal read directly; the 1977 GAO testimony is readable through web extraction.

## Limits and discrepancies requiring review

- The panel summary gives 14 deaths; the later Reclamation history and fact sheet give 11. The draft explicitly uses Reclamation’s eleven and does not invent a reconciliation. It is not described as eleven drownings.
- The fact sheet compresses the failure time to 11 a.m.; the detailed project history gives crest collapse at 11:55 and breakthrough at 11:57. The draft uses the latter account.
- Slower filling alone (panel assessment) is not the same counterfactual as slower filling plus an available main outlet (GAO discussion).
- Two possible initiating erosion paths remain possible. The exact failed material was washed away. No diagram reconstructs an unobserved path as fact.
- Irrigation restoration and federal payments are separate recovery indicators; neither measures the full human or economic loss.
- The article does not evaluate present-day dam compliance. No new hydraulic calculations or causal percentages were generated.
- Large original PDFs and extracted text were removed after inspection. Sources.json retains URLs, exact passage locators and the panel file’s SHA-256 digest.
