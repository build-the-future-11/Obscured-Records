## Editorial rule

A story moves from **candidate** to **approved** only when:

- headline, date, names, counts and material causal claims are checked against exact source passages;
- facts, allegations, testimony, analysis and uncertainty are distinguished;
- any visual used has an adequate rights/provenance record;
- a named editor accepts the final copy;
- approval date/evidence is recorded;
- the final production rendering is checked.

URL reachability, AI review, repository tests or existing `status: "published"` metadata are not sufficient on their own.
