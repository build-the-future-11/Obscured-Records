# Walkerton evidence packet

An AI-assisted archival draft awaiting human review. The four sources supply distinct evidence: inquiry findings, contemporaneous epidemiology, original laboratory research, and later operational guidance. Exact inspected locations and bounded claim mappings are in sources.json.

## Limits

- The inquiry source is its official summary reproduced by a water-sector association. Ontario archive URLs redirected, and the linked archive replay was rate-limited. The mirror is disclosed in the draft; do not describe it as the full inquiry.
- Early and later fatality figures are preserved as dated findings; the reason for the revision has not been verified.
- The molecular paper does not establish every individual's transmission route. Negative samples do not reconstruct earlier water conditions.
- Guidance supplies context, not a retrospective compliance ruling or measurement of current safety.
- No diagram is supplied: an apparent single linear chain would blur the distinction among source attribution, clinical evidence and notification.
- No primary-source images or quotations are reproduced. Human factual, technical and rights review remain open.
