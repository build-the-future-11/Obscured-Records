# Sample 01 — When the interface says one thing and the system does another

**Status:** editorial planning draft  
**Theme:** hidden assumptions crossing technical boundaries

## Subject
The numbers were right. The meaning was not.

## Preheader
Mars Climate Orbiter, Therac-25, and the failure modes hidden between components.

## Opening note
Some failures are easiest to remember as one-line mistakes. The more useful record often sits at the boundary: where a value changes hands, where a specification meets an implementation, or where one team assumes another has already checked the meaning.

## Three records worth understanding

### Mars Climate Orbiter
Internal candidate: `mars-climate-orbiter-interface` — do not send until approved.

Editorial angle: NASA’s mishap board documented a Newton-second versus pound-force-second mismatch in a ground software interface, then listed broader communication, staffing, transition, training and verification failures around it.

Primary source: NASA Mars Climate Orbiter Mishap Investigation Board Phase I Report.

### Therac-25
Public feature candidate: `therac-25`.

Editorial angle: use the existing record only after the current launch selection/claim review confirms it remains suitable for promotion.

### Ariane reused assumptions
Internal candidate: `ariane-reused-assumptions` — do not send until approved.

Editorial angle: compare reuse of software/assumptions only after the draft receives primary-source review.

## Overlooked item
A specification can exist and still fail as a control if it is not used in development, testing and integrated verification.

## Primary document
NASA Mars Climate Orbiter Mishap Investigation Board Phase I Report.

## From Obscured Records
Use an already-approved public feature at send time. Do not substitute an unapproved draft to fill the slot.

## Reader action
Invite corrections or primary-source leads related to engineering-interface failures.

# Sample 02 — When reported numbers become institutional facts

**Status:** editorial planning draft  
**Theme:** corporate controls, verification and trust

## Subject
A balance sheet is also a chain of trust.

## Preheader
Wirecard, Parmalat, Satyam and what happens when verification becomes ceremonial.

## Opening note
Financial records look authoritative because they are structured, repeated and signed. The interesting question is what happens when a number survives because each layer assumes another layer has already established that it is real.

## Three records worth understanding

### Wirecard

Use only after final derivative-image provenance and launch selection are cleared.

### Parmalat
Internal candidate: `parmalat-fictional-cash` — do not send until approved.

### Satyam
Internal candidate: `satyam-confession` — do not send until approved.

## Overlooked item
The editorial comparison should distinguish fraud from control weakness, audit failure, liquidity pressure and governance breakdown rather than compressing them into a generic “scandal” category.

## Primary document
Choose one regulator, court, audit or company filing that has been claim-checked for the final selected story.

## From Obscured Records
Use the strongest approved corporate-controls record available at send time.

# Sample 03 — The boundary of exposure

**Status:** editorial planning draft  
**Theme:** contamination, public health and the distance between source and harm

## Subject
The hazard did not stop at the fence.

## Preheader
Minamata, Goiânia, Bhopal and the pathways institutions failed to contain.

## Opening note
A facility boundary is a legal and operational line. It is not necessarily an environmental one. Water, food, dust, discarded equipment and ordinary human movement can carry hazards into places that were never treated as part of the original system.

## Three records worth understanding

### Minamata
Public feature candidate: `minamata-food-chain`.

### Goiânia
Public feature candidate: `goiania-blue-powder`.

### Bhopal
Internal candidate: `bhopal-where-the-shift-ends` — do not send until approved.

## Overlooked item
The editorial value is in tracing the route of exposure precisely. Avoid equating unlike toxicological mechanisms simply because all three stories involve contamination.

## Primary document
Select the strongest public-health or investigation source attached to the final approved story.

## From Obscured Records
Use the approved public feature with the cleanest claim and rights review.

## Reader action
Invite source leads for official reports, historical datasets and archival maps.

# Sample 04 — A warning is not yet a decision

**Status:** editorial planning draft  
**Theme:** anomaly detection, authority and institutional response

## Subject
Someone noticed. What happened next?

## Preheader
Challenger, Columbia, Petrov and the distance between warning and action.

## Opening note
A warning can be technically accurate and institutionally weak. It can arrive through the wrong channel, lack authority, conflict with a model, or be interpreted under time pressure. The record worth reconstructing is not only who saw the signal, but what the system allowed them to do with it.

## Three records worth understanding

### Challenger
Internal candidate: `challenger-warning-and-decision` — do not send until approved.

### Columbia
Internal candidate: `columbia-evidence-not-requested` — do not send until approved.

### Petrov
Internal candidate: `petrov-false-alarm` — do not send until approved.

## Overlooked item
Do not turn these cases into a single heroic-or-villainous template. The evidence, authority structures and consequences differ.

## Primary document
Use the relevant investigation or official historical record only after claim-level review.

## From Obscured Records
If no story in this theme is approved by send time, replace the entire issue theme rather than quietly promoting a draft.

## Reader action
Invite readers to send public primary sources or corrections about institutional warning systems.
