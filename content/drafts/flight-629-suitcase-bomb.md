United Air Lines Flight 629 departed Denver and broke apart minutes later, killing all 44 people aboard. Investigators found evidence of an explosion in checked baggage.

The FBI traced the bomb to Jack Gilbert Graham, who had placed it in his mother’s suitcase after buying travel insurance policies on her life. He confessed and was convicted.
