The Therac-25 delivered radiation for cancer treatment. Between 1985 and 1987, six known accidents involved severe overdoses. Nancy Leveson and Clark Turner’s investigation traces faults in the machine and the handling of injury reports.

## What happened

Some accidents involved timing-sensitive software faults. The machine relied more heavily on software safety controls than earlier designs, while confusing feedback and fragmented reporting made the incidents harder to recognize.

## Why this record matters

The case asks how a dangerous system remains constrained when its software or assumptions fail. It also shows why an inability to reproduce an injury report immediately does not establish that the equipment is safe.
