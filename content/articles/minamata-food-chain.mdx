In Minamata, Japan, severe neurological illness was officially reported in 1956 after abnormalities had already been observed in animals around the bay.

## What happened

Japanese official histories identify methylmercury compounds in wastewater from the Chisso factory as the cause, with exposure occurring through contaminated fish and shellfish. Establishing and acting on that causal link took years while exposure continued.

## Why this record matters

Minamata connects environmental measurements with patients’ lives and decisions about protection. The later mercury treaty is part of its legacy, but does not resolve every dispute over recognition, compensation or responsibility.
