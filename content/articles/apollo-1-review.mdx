Virgil I. Grissom, Edward H. White II and Roger B. Chaffee died in a fire inside the Apollo spacecraft during a ground test at Launch Complex 34 on 27 January 1967.

## What happened

NASA’s Apollo 204 Review Board preserved findings, testimony and a reconstruction of the accident. Its report describes a test environment with a high-pressure oxygen atmosphere, combustible material and a command module whose hatch could not be opened quickly under the conditions of the fire.

## Why this record matters

Apollo 1 is a case about how a review distinguishes a trigger from the conditions that let it become fatal. The official investigation is evidence from the institution that conducted it; this AI-assisted brief does not substitute for every personal account of the loss.
