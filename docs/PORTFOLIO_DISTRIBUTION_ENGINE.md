# Obscured Portfolio Distribution Engine

Start this only after the relaunch candidate in issue #4 is certified.

## Purpose

Obscured Records should turn verified technical work into clear public communication without changing the underlying claim.

Each release bundle has six outputs:

1. canonical technical source;
2. research explainer;
3. short social summary;
4. one figure/visual with source provenance;
5. newsletter item;
6. optional interview/audio adaptation.

## Editorial tracks

### Research Explainers
600–1,200 words. Explain the problem, method, evidence, limitations, and why the result matters. Negative/mixed results stay negative/mixed.

### Frontier Briefing
A weekly digest of work that actually changed during the week. Do not fill space with unverified roadmap claims.

### Builder / Researcher Interviews
Focus on concrete decisions, failed approaches, evidence, and next gates rather than generic founder profiles.

### Data Stories
One defensible chart/figure plus a compact interpretation and the source artifact behind it.

## First four assignments

### 1. LAM-JEPA
Angle: what a bounded negative/inconclusive result can still teach about quantized predictive representations.
Source requirement: canonical release/manuscript + claim/evidence ledger.
Do not imply venue acceptance or stronger general JEPA conclusions.

### 2. IRIS / Space Intelligence
Angle: source-population shift and why astronomical detection systems need reproducible evaluation.
Source requirement: completed, unlocked evidence only.
Do not disclose or imply outcomes from still-locked evaluation lanes.

### 3. NeuroCAD
Angle: from language/feature intent to exact CAD and simulation-ready geometry.
Source requirement: exact-CAD and meshing evidence as those gates land.
Do not infer mechanical or safety validity from geometry/mesh success.

### 4. FinanceBench
Angle: why financial ML needs point-in-time data, leakage controls, walk-forward evaluation, regimes, and cost sensitivity.
Source requirement: FinanceBench v0 specification + reference implementation/example.
Do not describe simulated evidence as live trading performance.

## Release checklist

Before publication:
- identify canonical source revision;
- copy every quantitative claim into a claim ledger;
- identify each figure's source artifact;
- confirm project status and venue status;
- state limitations prominently;
- remove private applicant/user/customer data;
- run link check and preview;
- record publication URL and date.

## 30-day cadence

Week 1:
- certify relaunch;
- prepare LAM-JEPA and IRIS explainers;
- draft briefing template.

Week 2:
- publish/prepare NeuroCAD explainer when exact-CAD evidence exists;
- first researcher interview;
- weekly briefing.

Week 3:
- FinanceBench explainer;
- second interview;
- weekly briefing.

Week 4:
- synthesis issue: what changed across Bu1LD/FinanceMeta/VertexED/NeuroCAD;
- weekly briefing;
- review reader/subscriber evidence and next editorial queue.

## Success measures

Track:
- published evidence-backed pieces;
- newsletter sends only when delivery is actually verified;
- unique readers/subscribers where measurement is available;
- qualified replies/inbound collaboration;
- click-through to canonical technical artifacts;
- correction rate / claim-audit failures.

Do not use raw social impressions as a substitute for readership or research impact.
